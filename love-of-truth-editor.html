APPLICATION INFORMATION

Love of Truth Editor

Michael Grasso’s private desktop app for editing and publishing videos for the Love of Truth channel.

How the editor uses YouTube

The editor uses YouTube API Services to show information about the connected channel, display recent aggregate analytics, and check the video schedule. With the operator’s approval, it uploads a finished video and its title, description, visibility, schedule, and thumbnail as applicable.

The operator connects the channel through Google’s consent screen in the desktop app. The operator reviews and approves each upload in the app.

Read the Love of Truth Editor privacy notice for details on data use, storage, and how to revoke access.

Application use

The editor is for the operator’s own channel. The operator is responsible for reviewing the video, its audience settings, and the publication schedule. Use of the YouTube features is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google explains how it handles information in the Google Privacy Policy.
