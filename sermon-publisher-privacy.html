New Covenant Sermon Publisher — Privacy Notice

Updated November 3, 2026

New Covenant Sermon Publisher is a private tool used by authorized New Covenant OPC staff to prepare and schedule church sermon broadcasts and their accompanying website entries. It is operated by Michael Grasso. This notice concerns that application, rather than the general operation of the church’s website or LoveOfTruth.net.

Information the application accesses

When an authorized staff member connects the church’s Google account, the application uses YouTube API Services to access the church’s YouTube channel details and broadcast information, including broadcast identifiers, titles, descriptions, scheduled times, links, and publishing status. It also works with staff-provided sermon outlines and the church’s configured service information.

The application uses Google authorization credentials to access the connected account. It does not ask staff to give the application their Google account password.

How information is used and shared

The application uses this access to read the broadcast schedule and create or update approved sermon broadcast titles, descriptions, and scheduled times. Approved sermon descriptions and YouTube links may also appear on the church website and LoveOfTruth.net through their publishing and hosting services.

Sermon descriptions may be drafted from staff-provided outlines using the staff member’s authorized AI account. Staff review the text before publication. Google account credentials are not included in the AI drafting input.

The church does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Google’s processing of information through its services is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Storage and retention

Google authorization credentials and publishing records are saved in the operator’s configured local workspace for this workflow. That workspace may be included in the operator’s device backups or file synchronization settings. Saved publishing records support review, publication history, and recovery from interrupted operations.

Authorized staff can request removal of locally saved authorization and publishing records by contacting the church. Disconnecting access does not remove broadcasts or website entries that were already published.

Revoke access or request deletion

Staff can revoke the application’s Google access through Google Account third-party connections. To request deletion of locally saved authorization or publishing records, or to ask a question about privacy, contact newcovenantemail@gmail.com.

This information page

This page contains no sign-in form, video player, or analytics script. The website’s hosting service may process ordinary web-request information when serving the page.
