Love of Truth Editor Privacy Notice

Love of Truth Editor is a private desktop video editor operated by Michael Grasso. This notice covers the editor’s use of Google and YouTube information.

What the editor accesses

When the operator authorizes the editor through Google, it uses YouTube API Services to read the connected channel’s identifier, name, and available subscriber count. It reads aggregate YouTube Analytics, including views and watch time, and checks video identifiers, titles, scheduled times, and publishing status. Google provides authorization tokens for these requests through its own sign-in screen.

How the editor uses and shares information

The editor uses this information to identify the channel, display metrics, show the publishing calendar, and confirm upload and scheduling results. When the operator approves a YouTube post, the editor sends the selected finished video, posting information, and applicable thumbnail to YouTube. YouTube handles the video according to the visibility and timing selected by the operator.

The editor can separately use the operator’s authorized AI account to draft posting text from a local video transcript. Google authorization tokens stay out of that drafting input. Google’s processing of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Storage and retention

When the operator enables Remember, the editor saves authorization tokens in macOS Keychain so it can renew access. Otherwise it holds the authorization for the current app session. The editor keeps local publishing history and schedule information so the operator can check results and avoid duplicate uploads. A snapshot of the channel’s subscriber count can appear in the operator’s private synced Love of Truth dashboard.

Local publishing history remains available until it is removed from the app’s local data. Schedule information refreshes when the editor checks YouTube.

Revoke access or request deletion

Use Disconnect & forget saved in the editor’s YouTube connection settings to remove the saved authorization from the Mac. To revoke the editor’s Google grant, use Google Account third-party connections. Videos already uploaded are managed in YouTube Studio. To request deletion of saved app information or ask a privacy question, use the Love of Truth contact page.
