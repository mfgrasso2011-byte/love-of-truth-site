“It seemed that a ray of light had broken through the clouds in the middle of a storm at sea. A wave of comfort swept over him. He felt like the crushing weight had been lifted from his shoulders. God would forgive him. He understood. He knew it to be true.”

Far to the west lies the land of Chayah, a land of hope for all who are willing to make the desperate journey across the sea. Nathan Ashir and his family, however, had no thought of leaving their home. They had everything they needed. His family was the most powerful family in all Beth Aven until he lost everything. As his family is brought lower and lower, they must learn to place their trust in the one who is sovereign over all.

““Dr. Michael Grasso has written an engaging book that shares the gospel in a many-faceted way. Experienced readers will get a whiff of John Bunyan's Pilgrim's Progress. However, what's different here is that the biblical doctrine comes through more in the drama itself than in the conversations, as Bunyan typically does. In addition, Grasso focuses more on the evangelistic point in time than on the journey of the Christian. An excellent choice for family devotions, I would recommend that the reader look up all the people names, place names, and biblical references, to get the full scope of what is offered here. The reader hopes a second book detailing the voyage will come soon, carrying the story further.””

~ Lane Keister
PhD in Biblical Studies, Pastor of Momence Orthodox Presbyterian Church
